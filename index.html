About me

Hi everyone! This is Hasan. I am a compiuter engineering PhD student in ECpE Department at Iowa State University. My research focuses on discovering spatial-temporal patterns from moving object data. Currently, I am designing graph neural network-based link prediction models for evolving graph networks. I am also collaborating with chemists from two different institutions where I develop tools for analyzing large-scale molecular data.

Projects

  • Graph Neural Network-based Link Prediction Models for Evolving Graph Networks
    Developed a novel graph neural network-based link prediction model for evolving graph networks. The model is designed to predict the future links in the graph network.

  • Generalization Aware Compression
    Developed a novel compression algorithm that is aware of the generalization of the data. The algorithm is designed to compress the data in a way that it can be used for generalization tasks.

  • Large-scale Molecular Data Analysis
    Developed a tool for analyzing large-scale molecular data. The tool is designed to analyze the data and provide insights into the data.
